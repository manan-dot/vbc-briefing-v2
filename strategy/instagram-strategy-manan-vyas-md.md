# Instagram Strategy & Roadmap — Manan Vyas, MD

**Personal brand:** A physician who builds AI for medicine.
**Primary goal:** Authority / thought leadership at the intersection of clinical medicine and AI.
**Capacity:** ~4–6 hrs/week, comfortable with *some* talking-head video.
**Horizon:** 90-day sprint + 6–12 month authority arc.

> How to read this: the strategy sections define *what you stand for and how you show up*. The roadmap section tells you *exactly what to do, week by week*. Start with the positioning, then jump to the 90-day roadmap.

## 1. Positioning — the one thing you own

Most healthcare-AI content comes from **one of two camps**: engineers who've never been in an exam room, or clinicians who've never shipped code. You're the rare overlap — an **MD who actually builds and ships AI in production**, inside value-based care. That intersection *is* your moat. Don't dilute it by becoming "another AI account."

**Positioning statement (internal compass):**
> I help clinicians, founders, and operators understand what AI in medicine *actually* looks like — from inside the exam room *and* the codebase.

**Profile bio (pick/adapt one — keep it specific, drop the buzzwords):**
- `MD building AI for medicine 🩺⚙️ | What actually works in the clinic, not the hype | Value-based care + clinical AI`
- `Physician + AI builder. I ship the tools I wish I'd had as a doctor. Notes on clinical AI, honestly.`
- `Doctor who codes. Building AI for real medicine → fewer clicks, better care. Following along in public.`

**Profile setup checklist:**
- Name field = `Manan Vyas, MD` (the Name field is searchable — put "MD" and a keyword like "AI" here, not just in the bio).
- Category: `Digital creator` or `Science, Technology & Engineering`.
- One-tap **link in bio** (Linktree/beacons or a simple personal page): newsletter/LinkedIn, a "what I'm building" link, and 1 flagship post.
- Pinned grid (3 posts): (1) your POV/manifesto, (2) your best explainer, (3) a "who I am / why listen to me" post.
- Profile photo: clean, high-trust, recognizable. Same avatar across LinkedIn/X/IG.

**Guardrail — say what you are, and aren't:** You're sharing perspective, not practicing medicine on the internet. Add a light disclaimer in your bio link or highlights ("Educational, not medical advice"). See §9.

## 2. Authority thesis — your repeatable narrative

Authority comes from **saying the same true, non-obvious thing over and over**, from a place only you can speak from. Your throughline:

> **"AI won't replace doctors — but it's already changing what the job is. Here's what that actually looks like from the inside."**

Every post should ladder up to one of these sub-arguments:
1. **The gap between AI demos and clinical reality** — where tools break the moment they meet a real workflow, and why.
2. **What "good" looks like** — the design principles that make clinical AI actually safe, adopted, and useful (value-based care lens: outcomes, not clicks).
3. **Building in public** — what you're shipping, what you got wrong, what you learned. Founders and clinicians both love this.
4. **The clinician-builder path** — for the growing wave of doctors who want to build. You're a guide one step ahead of them.

If a post doesn't clearly serve one of these, it's a "nice post" — not an authority post. Cut it or bank it.

## 3. Content pillars (4) — with example hooks

Rotate these so no single week is all one flavor. Each pillar has example hooks you can reuse as templates.

### Pillar A — Clinical reality checks (your signature)
*Myth-bust AI hype with what actually happens in care.*
- "Everyone says AI will read every scan. Here's what happens when it meets a real ED at 2am."
- "3 things AI scribes get wrong that no demo will show you."
- "'AI will cut documentation time.' Sometimes. Here's when it doesn't."
- "The most overhyped clinical AI use case of 2026 — and the quietly underrated one."

### Pillar B — Explainers (grow reach + saves)
*Make one healthcare-AI or value-based-care concept click in 60 seconds.*
- "Value-based care in 45 seconds (and why AI matters for it)."
- "Ambient AI scribes, explained without the buzzwords."
- "Why 'accuracy' is the wrong way to judge a clinical AI tool."
- "What actually happens to your data when a hospital 'uses AI.'"

### Pillar C — Building in public (your credibility engine)
*Show the work. This is what makes you a builder, not a commentator.*
- "We shipped X this week. Here's the one thing that surprised me."
- "I was wrong about [assumption]. Here's what the data showed."
- "The unglamorous 80% of building healthcare AI nobody posts about."
- "A feature we killed — and why killing it was the right call."

### Pillar D — The clinician-builder path (community + inbound)
*Mentor the next wave of doctors who build.*
- "You're an MD who wants to build. Here's the first thing I'd do."
- "You don't need a CS degree to build in healthcare. You need this."
- "5 skills that transferred from medicine to building product."
- "How I think about medicine vs. building — and where they collide."

**Suggested weekly mix:** 1× A or B (reach), 1× C (credibility), and Stories/notes from D. Alternate the "reach" pillar week to week.

## 4. Voice & visual identity

**Voice:** Direct, evidence-grounded, a little contrarian, never cynical. You're the calm expert in a hype-filled room. Write like you'd talk to a smart colleague — no jargon walls, no LinkedIn-guru cadence, no "🚀 game-changer" language.

**Do:**
- Lead with a concrete claim or a scene ("It's 2am in the ED…").
- Use specifics: real workflows, real tradeoffs, real numbers when you can share them.
- Take a position. Nuance *after* the hook, not instead of it.
- Credit others; disagree respectfully with named ideas, not people.

**Don't:**
- Post generic "AI is transforming healthcare" filler (everyone does; it says nothing).
- Give medical advice or discuss identifiable patients/cases (see §9).
- Chase every trend — only trend-jack when you have a genuine clinical/builder take.

**Visual system (camera-light-friendly):** Pick **one** template family and reuse it so your grid is instantly recognizable.
- **Carousels:** one accent color + one clean font, big bold first slide (the hook), 6–8 slides, last slide = a single takeaway + soft CTA ("Save this / follow for more from inside clinical AI"). Build a reusable Canva template so each carousel takes ~30 min, not 2 hrs.
- **Reels:** simple talking-head, good light, captions burned in, hook in the first 2 seconds. No fancy editing required — clarity beats production.
- **Consistency > polish.** A recognizable, repeatable look compounds trust faster than one-off beautiful posts.

## 5. Format mix & batching for ~4–6 hrs/week

Realistic weekly output at this capacity: **3 feed posts + Stories most days + comment engagement.** Don't overcommit to daily Reels — consistency you can sustain for 6 months beats a 3-week sprint you abandon.

**Weekly output target:**
- **1 talking-head Reel** (Pillar A or B) — your reach + face-to-name driver.
- **2 carousels** (one explainer, one building-in-public) — your saves + authority driver.
- **Stories:** 3–5 days/week — behind-the-scenes, polls, reshares, quick takes. Low effort, high relationship value.

**Batching workflow (this is what makes 4–6 hrs work):**
- **90-min "capture" block (1×/week):** film 1–2 Reels back-to-back, same setup/outfit. Rough-script the hooks only; talk naturally.
- **90-min "build" block (1×/week):** turn 2 ideas into carousels using your template. Write captions here too.
- **~15 min/day "engage" block:** reply to every comment, leave 5–10 thoughtful comments on accounts in your niche (this is 50% of early growth — see §6).
- **Repurpose, don't reinvent:** every LinkedIn post, internal doc insight, or X thread you already write is a carousel or Reel script. Your day job *is* your content pipeline.

**Idea capture:** keep a running notes file. Every clinical friction, every build lesson, every "wait, people don't know this?" moment is a post. Aim to bank 2–3 weeks ahead so you never post from empty.

## 6. Growth engine — how authority actually compounds

Followers are a lagging indicator. These are the levers that build *authority* (reach among the right people + inbound), in priority order:

1. **Instagram SEO (highest-leverage, most ignored).** IG search now indexes captions, on-screen text, and the Name field.
   - Put keywords in your **Name field** and first caption line ("clinical AI," "AI in medicine," "value-based care").
   - Write the **first sentence** of every caption as a searchable, plain-language statement.
   - Add **alt text** to carousels/images describing the topic.
   - Use **5–10 specific niche hashtags** (#healthtech #clinicalAI #digitalhealth #physicianentrepreneur #healthcareAI) over giant generic ones. Mix medium (#healthtech) and small/niche tags where you can actually rank.
2. **Engagement-led reach (your first 90 days' main growth source).** Spend your 15 min/day commenting substantively on ~15 target accounts: healthcare-AI founders, physician creators, digital-health VCs, med-Twitter/IG crossover voices. Thoughtful comments on bigger accounts put you in front of exactly your audience — and often earn the follow-back and DM relationship.
3. **Collaborations & podcast clips (your inbound flywheel).** Authority accelerates when *other* credible people platform you.
   - Turn any podcast/panel/webinar you do into 2–3 Reel clips (with permission).
   - Do IG **collab posts** (co-authored) with one aligned creator/founder per month — instant reach into their audience.
   - Guest on 1 podcast/space per month; clip it for IG.
4. **Cross-post leverage.** You likely already post on LinkedIn/X. Repurpose your best-performing thoughts there into IG carousels/Reels. Drive your LinkedIn/X audience to IG periodically ("I'm posting the visual version of these on Instagram").
5. **DM strategy (relationships, not spam).** When someone thoughtful engages, reply and, where natural, move to a real conversation. Warm DMs with founders, clinicians, and journalists are where speaking invites and press actually come from. Never cold-pitch; always add value first.

**What to ignore:** follower-count vanity, engagement pods, buying reach, posting 2×/day to "beat the algorithm." None build durable authority.

## 8. Metrics that matter (authority, not vanity)

Review these every **Sunday in ~20 minutes**. Track in a simple sheet; watch trends, not single posts.

**Primary KPIs (authority signals):**
- **Saves + shares per post** — the truest signal your content is *valuable*, not just seen. Target: your saves/reach ratio trending up week over week.
- **Profile-visit → follow rate** — is your profile converting the curious? (Reach → profile visits → follows.)
- **Reach among target segments** — anecdotally track *who* follows/engages (clinicians, founders, investors, journalists). Quality > quantity.
- **Inbound quality** — count meaningful DMs, collab asks, podcast/press/speaking inquiries per month. This is the real goal.

**Secondary (health checks):** follower growth rate, Reel watch-through, comment volume, link taps.

**Weekly review prompts:**
- Which post got the most **saves**? Why? Do more of that.
- Did I get any **inbound** worth pursuing? Reply within 48 hrs.
- What did I learn this week that's a post? Bank it.
- Am I on cadence? If I fell behind, what's the *smallest* sustainable version?

## 9. Guardrails for an MD posting on healthcare AI

Non-negotiables that protect your license, your credibility, and your patients:
- **No medical advice.** Educate on concepts; never diagnose, treat, or advise individuals. Keep an "educational, not medical advice" note in your bio link/highlights.
- **No identifiable patients or cases.** No PHI, ever. Composite/illustrative scenarios only, clearly framed as such.
- **Disclose affiliations.** Be clear when you're talking about work/your company vs. general commentary. Follow your employer's social/comms policy and any conflict-of-interest norms.
- **Stay in your lane of evidence.** Distinguish "here's what studies show" from "here's my opinion." Your credibility *is* the brand — protect it by never overclaiming.
- **Compliance-aware framing.** Healthcare AI touches regulation (FDA, HIPAA, reimbursement). When you reference it, be accurate or say "as I understand it." Correct yourself publicly when wrong — it builds trust.
- **Professional tone on hard topics.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume clinicians, founders, and journalists are all reading.

## 10. 90-Day Roadmap

Three 30-day phases. Each has a theme, weekly milestones, and **exit criteria** (don't rush to the next phase until you hit them).

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–30): *Set the stage, find your voice*
**Theme:** Get the profile right, establish cadence, learn what resonates. Growth is *not* the goal yet — reps and clarity are.

- **Week 1:** Finalize bio, Name field, avatar, link-in-bio, and highlights. Draft your positioning + authority thesis (§1–2). Build your **carousel template** + set up a filming spot. Write down 15 post ideas.
- **Week 2:** Publish your **manifesto/POV post** (pin it) + first explainer carousel. Film your first Reel. Start the daily 15-min engagement habit with 15 target accounts. Post 3 feed items this week.
- **Week 3:** Full cadence live (1 Reel + 2 carousels + Stories). Publish a "who I am / why listen to me" post. Bank 2 weeks of ideas. Begin cross-posting your best LinkedIn/X thoughts.
- **Week 4:** Ship your first **building-in-public** post. Do your first Sunday review with real data. Identify your 2 best-performing posts and note *why*.

**Exit criteria:** Profile fully optimized • 10–12 posts published • daily engagement is a habit • you know which pillar resonates most • 2+ weeks of banked ideas.

### Phase 2 — Momentum (Days 31–60): *Sharpen and amplify*
**Theme:** Double down on what worked, start collaborations, begin the inbound flywheel.

- **Week 5:** Analyze Phase 1. Reallocate toward your top pillar. Refine hooks based on saves/shares data.
- **Week 6:** Land your **first collaboration** — a collab post or podcast/space guest slot with one aligned creator/founder. Clip it for Reels.
- **Week 7:** Publish a "spicier" reality-check Reel with a clear POV (your Pillar A signature). Start warm DM relationships with 5 people who engage thoughtfully.
- **Week 8:** Second collaboration or guest appearance. Review inbound: any collab/podcast/press asks? Respond within 48 hrs. Sunday review: is profile-visit→follow rate improving?

**Exit criteria:** 2+ collaborations done • ≥1 piece of meaningful inbound • a clear signature format that reliably earns saves • repeatable batching rhythm you're not dreading.

### Phase 3 — Authority (Days 61–90): *Become a name people cite*
**Theme:** Convert reach into recognition and inbound. You're now a known voice in the niche.

- **Week 9:** Publish a **flagship** piece — your most ambitious explainer or a strong contrarian take with data. Promote it across LinkedIn/X.
- **Week 10:** Pursue 1 podcast guest spot and 1 collab intentionally (pitch with value, don't cold-spam). Turn a work insight into a "here's what we learned building X" post.
- **Week 11:** Engage the wave — reply to every comment, deepen DM relationships, look for a journalist/newsletter to build a relationship with.
- **Week 12:** **90-day retrospective.** What compounded? Codify your winning formats into templates. Set the next quarter's 1–2 goals (see §11).

**Exit criteria:** A recognizable POV people associate with you • ≥1 podcast/press/speaking conversation in motion • a documented "what works for me" playbook • a sustainable system you can run indefinitely.

## 11. 6–12 month authority arc

Once the 90-day system is running, authority is about **stacking credibility milestones**. Rough sequence (don't force the timeline — these are targets, not deadlines):

- **Months 4–6:** Consistent signature content; 2–3 podcast/panel appearances; first press mention or newsletter feature; a small but genuinely engaged audience of clinicians/founders/investors. Consider starting a lightweight **newsletter** as your owned channel (IG builds reach; email builds a durable list).
- **Months 7–9:** Recognized as "the MD who builds AI" in your corner of the internet; regular inbound (collabs, podcasts, advisory pings); maybe your first **speaking slot** at a health-tech/AI event. Begin turning your best content into a signature framework or "point of view" people quote.
- **Months 10–12:** Inbound > outbound. You're getting invited, not pitching. Optionality opens up — speaking, advisory, a bigger platform for whatever you build next. Reassess: is IG still the right primary channel, or a feeder into something bigger (newsletter, podcast, your own venture's brand)?

**Leading indicators to watch (they move before follower count):** rising saves/shares ratio, more DMs from credible people, being tagged/cited by others, unsolicited collab and podcast asks, journalists in your audience.

Own the rare overlap — an **MD who ships AI in real medicine** — and say one true, non-obvious thing repeatedly: *AI is already changing what being a doctor means, and here's what that actually looks like from the inside.* Post **1 Reel + 2 carousels a week** across four pillars (reality checks, explainers, building-in-public, the clinician-builder path), spend **15 minutes a day** engaging with the right 15 accounts, and turn every podcast/collab into clips. Measure **saves, shares, and inbound quality — not followers.** Run the 90-day Foundation → Momentum → Authority roadmap, protect your credibility with clear medical/professional guardrails, and let inbound (podcasts, press, speaking) compound over 6–12 months.
