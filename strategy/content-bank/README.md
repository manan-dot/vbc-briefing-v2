## How to use this bank

1. **Follow the rhythm:** Mon = explainer carousel (Pillar B) · Wed = talking-head Reel (Pillar A) · Fri = building-in-public carousel (Pillar C). Stories prompts are listed per week.
2. **Fill the placeholders before posting.** Anything marked `[YOUR EXAMPLE: …]` is a slot for *your real* project, lesson, or number — especially in Pillar C posts. Nothing in this bank should be published with a claim that isn't yours. If you don't have a real example for a slot, swap in a post from the Week-1 idea bank instead.
3. **Batch per the strategy (§5):** film Wednesday Reels in one weekly 90-min capture block (script hooks only, talk naturally); build both carousels in one 90-min build block.
4. **Phases 2–3 are defaults, not scripture.** The strategy says to double down on what your Phase-1 data shows. Each phase file opens with a recalibration note — swap posts freely, keep the cadence.
5. **Guardrails apply to every post (§9):** no medical advice, no identifiable patients (composite scenarios are flagged as such in the drafts), disclose affiliation when talking about your own company's space, hedge regulatory claims.

## Voice reminders (from §4)

Direct, evidence-grounded, a little contrarian, never cynical. Lead with a claim or a scene. Take a position *then* add nuance. No "🚀 game-changer" language, no guru cadence, no generic "AI is transforming healthcare" filler.

## Carousel production notes

- One accent color, one font, big bold hook on slide 1, 6–8 slides, final slide = takeaway + soft CTA. Build the Canva template once (Week 1) and reuse.
- Reels: talking-head, good light, captions burned in, hook in the first 2 seconds. Clarity beats production.
- Add the suggested **alt text** to every carousel/image (it's indexed by Instagram search).
