## The 15 ideas

### Pillar A — Clinical reality checks

1. **"3 things AI scribes get wrong that no demo will show you."**
   Angle: demo conditions vs. a real clinic — interruptions, code-switching patients, the "oh by the way" doorknob moment. → *Expanded: Week 2 Reel.*

2. **"Everyone says AI will read every scan. Here's what happens when it meets a real ED at 2am."**
   Angle: the gap between benchmark performance and 2am staffing, workflow, and liability reality. Composite scenario, flagged as such. → *Expanded: Week 3 Reel.*

3. **"'AI will cut documentation time.' Sometimes. Here's when it doesn't."**
   Angle: when review-and-edit takes longer than writing; automation that shifts work instead of removing it. → *Expanded: Week 4 Reel.*

4. **"The most overhyped clinical AI use case right now — and the quietly underrated one."**
   Angle: a ranked, opinionated take. Spicy but defensible. → *Expanded: Week 7 Reel (the "spicier" POV slot).*

5. **"Why 'accuracy' is the wrong way to judge a clinical AI tool."**
   Angle: calibration, workflow fit, and failure modes matter more than a headline accuracy number. → *Expanded: Week 5 Reel.*

### Pillar B — Explainers

6. **"Value-based care in 45 seconds (and why AI matters for it)."**
   Angle: pay-for-outcomes vs. pay-per-visit, in plain words; AI as the only way the math scales. → *Expanded: Week 2 carousel (your first explainer).*

7. **"Ambient AI scribes, explained without the buzzwords."**
   Angle: what actually happens between "doctor talks" and "note appears." → *Expanded: Week 4 Mon carousel.*

8. **"What actually happens to your data when a hospital 'uses AI.'"**
   Angle: de-identification, BAAs, where models run — accurate and hedged (§9). → *Expanded: Week 6 Mon carousel.*

9. **"Why your doctor stares at the screen instead of you."**
   Angle: the EHR click-burden story as the origin of clinical AI; empathy for both sides of the desk. → *Expanded: Week 8 Mon carousel.*

10. **"FDA-cleared AI: what that does — and doesn't — mean."**
    Angle: clearance vs. approval vs. "just software," as-I-understand-it framing (§9). → *Expanded: Week 10 Mon carousel.*

### Pillar C — Building in public

11. **"The unglamorous 80% of building healthcare AI nobody posts about."**
    Angle: data plumbing, edge cases, clinician trust — the real work. → *Expanded: Week 3 Fri carousel.*

12. **"I was wrong about [assumption]. Here's what changed my mind."**
    Angle: a real reversal from your build work — the fastest credibility builder there is. → *Expanded: Week 6 Fri carousel (placeholder slots).*

13. **"A feature we killed — and why killing it was the right call."**
    Angle: restraint as a product skill in healthcare; what "do no harm" means for software. → *Expanded: Week 9 Fri carousel (placeholder slots).*

### Pillar D — The clinician-builder path

14. **"You're an MD who wants to build. Here's the first thing I'd do."**
    Angle: don't quit, don't enroll in a CS degree — start by documenting the friction you see daily. → *Expanded: Week 5 Fri carousel.*

15. **"5 skills that transferred from medicine to building product."**
    Angle: differential diagnosis = debugging; informed consent = UX honesty; triage = prioritization. → *Expanded: Week 11 Mon carousel.*
